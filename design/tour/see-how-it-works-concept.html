See how ConfidantPM works

Five steps from a blank page to a plan you trust. Every screen below is the real app.

Try ConfidantPM free
1

Create a project in four short steps

Choose Waterfall, Agile or Hybrid, name it, set a priority and a go-live date. Pick Guided setup and the assistant drafts your Charter and Risk Log from a few questions.

2

Answer one question at a time

No blank chat box and no long forms. One question per screen, a progress bar, and a suggested answer drawn from what you have already told it. Press Tab to accept it.

3

Review the tasks it proposes

The assistant turns your Charter into a task list. Edit anything, untick what you do not want, and only the tasks you accept are saved. Accepted tasks are yours: the AI never changes them.

4

See the plan on a Gantt chart

Dependencies, milestones, a Today line and the critical path in amber. Export to Excel or PDF to share with stakeholders.

5

Keep everyone informed

Log a weekly status update in three boxes, and let the assistant propose Exec and Team comms. You accept or discard every version, and old versions are kept.
